# Existing Mods and Naming Evidence

Initial public-description review: **2026-10-26**. These are reported features, not installed-game verification or a complete mod census.

| Project | Reported relevant behavior | Difference from Extra Sermons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Pray the Day Away](https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper/mods/72?tab=description) | Daily and repeated sermons; repeat costs can consume a prayer, risk downgrading its quality, or be disabled. | Extra Sermons proposes buying one-use permission through Royal Services with money. |
| [ZombieEnhanced](https://github.com/saveroo/ZombieEnhanced) | Includes a sermon-at-will feature among many other changes. | No Royal Services permit purchase is established by the reviewed description. |

No exact match for the Royal Services one-use purchase was found in this bounded search. This does not prove that none exists. Compatibility, current runtime functionality and implementation details were not tested.

The word **sermon** directly names the service in existing mod descriptions and player discussions. **Prayer** also refers to the prayer item, which can make a title sound like additional prayer content rather than additional opportunities to preach. No Nexus query-volume or ranking data was available; search effectiveness is a naming judgment, not a measured result.

The user selected **Extra Sermons**. A longer Nexus title such as “Extra Sermons — Buy Additional Services” was proposed but has not been adopted.
